🦝 Midnight Snackdown — How to Raid

You are Rusty (left screen, red bandana) racing against rival raccoon Soot (right screen, blue bandana) inside a sleeping 8-room house. Find the Picnic Basket (🧺), pack all 4 Feast Dishes (🍕 Pizza, 🧀 Cheese, 🍯 Honey, 🐟 Fish), and escape through the Garden Gate on the East wall of the Back Porch before your clock runs out!

🎮 Controls

  • Move through rooms & doors WASD or Arrows
  • Rummage / Grab loot / Disarm trap Space or E
  • Paw-Swat rival (same room) F or Q
  • Hold Counter-Tool in paw 1 – 5 (0 none)
  • Plant Booby Trap Shift + 1 – 5
  • Open Instructions / Options I / O / Esc

🗺️ House Ring Layout & Rules

Without the Picnic Basket, you can only carry 1 dish at a time. Once you find the Basket, every dish you find goes straight inside!
